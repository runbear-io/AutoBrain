# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome participation that is respectful, specific, and focused on
improving the project. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and
deliberate exposure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through
the security contact configured for the GitHub repository. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.
